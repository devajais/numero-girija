🧭 Reading Grid Positions

Understanding the 3×3 Grid Structure

Each position in the Lo Shu Grid has specific meanings and influences. Learning to read these positions helps you understand how different numbers in your birth date create unique personality patterns and life experiences.

Position 4

Mind & Logic

Intellectual abilities, analytical thinking, problem-solving, and mental organization

Position 9

Spirituality & Ideals

Higher consciousness, spiritual growth, humanitarian values, and universal love

Position 2

Emotions & Sensitivity

Emotional intelligence, intuition, relationships, and caring nature

Position 3

Creativity & Expression

Creative talents, self-expression, communication, and artistic abilities

Position 5

Balance & Center

Core stability, adaptability, change management, and finding equilibrium

Position 7

Intuition & Analysis

Inner wisdom, research abilities, philosophical thinking, and self-reflection

Position 8

Action & Willpower

Physical energy, determination, practical execution, and achievement drive

Position 1

Communication & Leadership

Speaking abilities, leadership qualities, self-confidence, and social interaction

Position 6

Material & Domestic

Material comfort, family life, responsibility, and practical matters

How to Interpret Your Grid

When you plot your birth date numbers onto the grid:

  • Filled Positions: Areas where you have natural strength and ability
  • Empty Positions: Missing numbers indicating growth opportunities
  • Multiple Numbers: Amplified energy in that life area (can be strength or excess)
