📝 Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Extract Digits from Date of Birth

Example DOB: 15-08-1947

  • • Day: 15 → 1, 5
  • • Month: 08 → 8 (ignore zeros)
  • • Year: 1947 → 1, 9, 4, 7

Step 2: Calculate Driver Number

Add digits of day until single digit:

15 → 1 + 5 = 6

Driver Number = 6 (represents your personality)

Step 3: Calculate Conductor Number

Add ALL digits (day + month + year) until single digit:

1 + 5 + 8 + 1 + 9 + 4 + 7 = 35
3 + 5 = 8

Conductor Number = 8 (represents your destiny)

Step 4: Fill the Grid

Count frequency of each number 1-9:

  • • Numbers present: 1 (×2), 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9
  • • Numbers missing: 2, 3

Mark present numbers in their grid positions and note missing numbers
