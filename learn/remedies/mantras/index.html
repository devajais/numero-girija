🕉️ Mantras & Chants for Each Number

Mantras are sacred sound vibrations that align you with planetary energies. Chanting mantras for your missing or weak numbers can strengthen their positive influence in your life. Each number corresponds to a planetary deity with specific mantras.

📿 How to Chant Mantras Properly

  1. Time: Chant during the recommended day and time for maximum benefit
  2. Place: Clean, quiet space facing the deity or east direction
  3. Preparation: Take bath, wear clean clothes, light a lamp/incense
  4. Posture: Sit comfortably with straight spine, use a mat or cushion
  5. Focus: Close eyes or gaze at deity image, concentrate on the planet
  6. Count: Use a mala (108 beads) to count repetitions
  7. Consistency: Daily practice is more powerful than occasional long sessions
  8. Faith: Chant with devotion, belief, and positive intention

🙏 Additional Spiritual Practices

Daily Sankalp (Intention Setting)

Before starting mantra chanting, set your intention:

"ॐ विष्णुः ॐ तत्सत् श्री गोविन्द गोविन्द गोविन्द"

Then state your wish mentally or aloud in your own words with pure heart.

Japa Mala (Prayer Beads)

  • Rudraksha Mala: All-purpose, especially for Sun, Mars, Saturn
  • Crystal/Sphatik Mala: Moon, Venus, Mercury
  • Tulsi Mala: All planets, general spiritual practice
  • Sandalwood Mala: Venus, Moon, peaceful vibrations

Completion Ritual (After 40 Days)

  1. Perform the final 108 chants with extra devotion
  2. Offer flowers, fruits, or sweets to the deity
  3. Light a ghee lamp and incense
  4. Donate items related to the planet (see lifestyle guide)
  5. Thank the divine for blessings received

Combining with Other Remedies

For maximum benefit, combine mantra practice with:

  • Wearing the appropriate gemstone
  • Following lifestyle dos and don'ts
  • Observing fasts on the planetary day
  • Charity and donations specific to the planet
