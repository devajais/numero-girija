💎 Gemstones & Crystals for Each Number

Gemstones are powerful tools in numerology for strengthening the vibrations of specific numbers. Each number is associated with a ruling planet, and wearing the corresponding gemstone can amplify positive traits and mitigate challenges.

⚠️ Important Guidelines

  • Consult a qualified numerologist or astrologer before wearing any gemstone
  • Always test gemstones for 3 days before permanent wearing
  • Blue Sapphire (Number 8) should be tested very carefully - it can have strong effects
  • Buy certified, natural gemstones from reputable sources
  • Cleanse and energize gemstones before wearing (sunlight/moonlight for 24 hours)

💡 Gemstone Care & Activation

Cleansing Your Gemstone

Before first use and monthly thereafter:

  • Soak in salt water for 3 hours
  • Rinse with clean water
  • Place in sunlight (for warm stones) or moonlight (for cool stones) for 24 hours

Energizing Ritual

On the recommended day (see each number above):

  • Wake up early, take a bath
  • Place gemstone in front of deity or sacred space
  • Chant the associated mantra 108 times
  • Wear the gemstone while continuing the mantra

When to Avoid Gemstones

  • During pregnancy (consult doctor first)
  • If you feel discomfort, headaches, or negative dreams
  • During major illness or surgery
  • If the number's planet is already very strong in your chart
