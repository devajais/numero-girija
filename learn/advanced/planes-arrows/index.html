⚡ Planes & Arrows in Lo Shu Grid

Beyond individual numbers, the Lo Shu Grid reveals patterns called Planes and Arrows. These show how you think, feel, and act—providing deeper insights into personality and life approach.

Three Planes of Existence

🧠 Mental Plane (Top Row: 4-9-2)

Represents: Thinking, logic, spirituality, emotions

Strong (2+ numbers): Intellectual, analytical, good memory, philosophical

Weak (0-1 numbers): Scattered thinking, poor planning, impulsive decisions

Remedies: Reading, puzzles, meditation, education

❤️ Emotional Plane (Middle Row: 3-5-7)

Represents: Feelings, creativity, adaptability, intuition

Strong (2+ numbers): Emotionally balanced, creative, good relationships

Weak (0-1 numbers): Emotional instability, relationship issues, sensitivity

Remedies: Arts, music, counseling, emotional expression

💪 Practical Plane (Bottom Row: 8-1-6)

Represents: Action, material world, physical work, execution

Strong (2+ numbers): Hard-working, grounded, achieves goals, practical

Weak (0-1 numbers): Lazy, procrastinates, poor execution, unrealistic

Remedies: Exercise, manual work, goal-setting, discipline

Arrows of Strength

🏆 Arrow of Success (4-5-6)

Left column - Determination, achievement, material success

📋 Arrow of Planning (2-5-8)

Right column - Strategic thinking, organization, foresight

💎 Arrow of Willpower (1-5-9)

Horizontal middle - Strong determination, never gives up

🎓 Arrow of Intellect (4-9-2)

Top row - Sharp mind, academic success, wisdom

🕉️ Arrow of Spirituality (3-5-7)

Middle row - Mystical abilities, healing, intuition

⚡ Arrow of Activity (8-1-6)

Bottom row - High energy, practical execution, results

💗 Arrow of Compassion (3-6-9)

Right diagonal - Service, helping others, empathy

😤 Arrow of Frustration (4-5-6)

Left diagonal - Impatience, anger if blocked

Arrows of Weakness (Missing Lines)

Missing 1-4-7 (Left Column)

Effect: Lack of determination, gives up easily, poor willpower

Remedy: Set small goals, build discipline, chant Mars/Ketu mantras

Missing 2-5-8 (Right Column)

Effect: Poor planning, disorganized, lack of foresight

Remedy: Use planners, create routines, Moon/Mercury mantras

Missing 3-5-7 (Middle Row)

Effect: Emotional emptiness, relationship struggles, no creativity

Remedy: Therapy, arts, Jupiter/Ketu mantras

Missing 4-8, 1-6, 3-9, 2-7 (Corners)

Effect: Depends on specific corner—analysis/action/spiritual/emotional gaps

Remedy: Strengthen missing number's planet through gemstones

How to Interpret Planes & Arrows

  1. Count numbers in each plane - Identify which planes are strong/weak
  2. Look for complete lines - Any row, column, or diagonal with all numbers present is an "arrow"
  3. Note missing lines - Empty lines show areas of challenge
  4. Balance is key - Ideally 2-3 numbers per plane; too many also creates imbalance
  5. Apply remedies - Strengthen weak planes through targeted practices
