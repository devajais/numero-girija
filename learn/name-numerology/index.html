📝 Name Numerology

Your name is more than just an identifier—it's a vibration that influences how people perceive you and what you attract in life. Learn how to calculate, understand, and optimize your name for success.

What is Name Numerology?

Name numerology assigns numerical values to the letters in your name. When you add these values together and reduce them to a single digit (1-9), you get your Name Number. This number reveals the energy and impression your name creates in the world.

Key Point: Your name number affects how others perceive you and what opportunities you attract, while your Driver and Conductor (from DOB) represent who you truly are inside.

Name Correction - How to Improve Your Name

1. Add a Letter

Add letters with values 1, 3, or 5 (positive energies) at the beginning or end of your name.

Example: JOHN → AJOHN or JOHNA

2. Change Spelling

Modify spelling while keeping pronunciation: Use Y for I, C for K, add silent letters.

Example: KARAN → KARANN, PRIYA → PRIYAA

3. Use Middle Name

Include or exclude your middle name in signatures and official documents.

Example: John Smith → John A Smith

4. Adopt a Nickname

Use a nickname with a favorable number in daily life and social media.

Example: Rajesh → Raj, Elizabeth → Liz

⚠️ Important: For name correction to work, you must use the new spelling consistently for at least 40 days in signatures, social media, and introductions. Legal name change is optional but more powerful.
