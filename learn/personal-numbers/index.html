Understanding Personal Numbers

Your Lo Shu Grid reveals three critical numbers that shape your life journey: Driver, Conductor, and Personal Year. Each plays a unique role in your personality and destiny.

1

Driver Number

Your Personality & Natural Tendencies

📌 What is it?

The Driver number is calculated from your birth day only. It represents your core personality, natural behavior, and how you approach life intuitively.

🧮 Calculation

Add digits of your birth day until you get a single digit:

Example: Born on 23rd
2 + 3 = 5
Driver Number = 5

🎯 What it reveals

  • • Your natural personality and behavior
  • • How you instinctively react to situations
  • • Your strengths and natural talents
  • • Most active until age 35-40
2

Conductor Number

Your Destiny & Life Path

📌 What is it?

The Conductor number is calculated from your complete date of birth. It represents your destiny, life purpose, and the path you're meant to follow.

🧮 Calculation

Add ALL digits from day, month, and year:

Example: 23-05-1990
2+3+0+5+1+9+9+0 = 29
2+9 = 11
1+1 = 2
Conductor Number = 2

🎯 What it reveals

  • • Your life purpose and destiny
  • • What life is pushing you towards
  • • Your career and relationship patterns
  • • Becomes more active after age 35-40
3

Personal Year Number

Your Current Year Energy

📌 What is it?

Your Personal Year number changes every year and reveals the specific energy and themes you'll experience during that year. It's calculated using your birth day, month, and the current year.

🧮 Calculation

Add your birth day + birth month + current year:

Example: Born 23-05, Year 2026
Day: 2+3 = 5
Month: 0+5 = 5
Year: 2+0+2+6 = 10 → 1+0 = 1
5+5+1 = 11 → 1+1 = 2
Personal Year 2026 = 2

🎯 What it reveals

  • • The theme and energy of the current year
  • • What opportunities and challenges to expect
  • • Best actions and decisions for the year
  • • Part of a 9-year cycle (1-9) that repeats

⚖️ Driver-Conductor Relationship

The relationship between your Driver and Conductor numbers is crucial:

✓ Friendly: Harmonious life, personality and destiny support each other
~ Neutral: Balanced energy, requires conscious effort
⚠ Opposite: Internal conflicts but potential for great growth
