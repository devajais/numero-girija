  • ✓ Wear ruby gemstone (minimum 3 carats) in gold ring on ring finger (consult astrologer for proper procedure)

  • ✓ Donate wheat, jaggery, or red cloth on Sundays
  • ✓ Offer water to Sun at sunrise facing east (fill copper vessel with water, add red flower and jaggery, offer while chanting mantras)
  • ✓ Use number 1 in phone number, vehicle number, or house number
  • ✓ Keep a picture or idol of Lord Surya (Sun God) in east direction of home
  • ✓ Strengthen relationship with father - seek his blessings
  • ✓ Practice sun salutation (Surya Namaskar) every morning
  • ✓ Avoid black color; wear more red, orange, and gold colors
  • ✓ Fast on Sundays (can take fruits and milk)
  • ✓ Feed jaggery and wheat to cows on Sundays

  • Om Suryaya Namaha (108 times daily)

  • Aditya Hridayam on Sundays
  • Om Ghrini Suryaya Namaha (for health and confidence)
